Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the consultation really cost nothing?

Yes. It doesn't cost anything to talk to us. We'll review your ticket and explain your options before you commit to anything.

Should I just pay my ticket?

Paying a ticket is the same as pleading guilty. The conviction and any demerit points go on your record and your insurer can see it. Talk to us first.

Do I have to go to court?

In many cases we can appear on your behalf. We'll let you know in your consultation if your attendance is needed.

Do you handle tickets in Innisfil and Bradford?

Yes. We regularly help drivers from Barrie, Innisfil, Bradford and all of Simcoe County, and we can represent you anywhere in Ontario.

I missed my court date. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. Reopenings and appeals are among our specialties. Contact us as soon as possible.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, cheque, certified cheque, money order, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and electronic funds transfer.
